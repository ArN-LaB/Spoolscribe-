# Validation — SpoolScribe v0.5.0 pre-release

Historical [v0.5.0 pre-release](https://github.com/ArN-LaB/Spoolscribe-/releases/tag/v0.5.0)
record. At publication v0.3.5 remained stable and v0.4.0's files were unchanged.

## Scope

This development minor release added local CIEDE2000 search with inclusive
thresholds, in-card personal editing, compact value comparison, consistent
filters/accordions, documented printer photos and clearer export. It extended
neither OpenSpool/OpenTag3D encoders nor hardware compatibility. Autonomous QR
and a Spoolman connection remained separate work.

### Preparation failures

The [first remote matrix](https://github.com/ArN-LaB/Spoolscribe-/actions/runs/41587919829)
passed Windows/macOS but failed both Linux versions. Its skipped builds are not
counted as successes. Extra margin/rounding imposed a 282-pixel sidebar minimum
on Linux; the correction uses actual margins and an even width for button
alignment. The reveal-file test had assumed Finder/Explorer; it now checks all
three commands (file selection on macOS/Windows, containing folder on Linux)
without launching a real program. GUI fixtures also isolate Orca so installed
profiles cannot silently fill a value. No test or supported platform was disabled.

## Delta E and personal copies

Delta E is not a percentage. Maximum 3 means `Delta E ≤ 3` with no added margin.
`C8E6A0` / `D8EEA3` gives 3.035999: excluded at 3, included at 10. Upward rounding
to hundredths does not hide exceedance; filtering keeps six-decimal precision.
Declared HEX proximity is not a physical printed/glossy/translucent/effect measurement.

Editing/details are exclusive; a dirty draft cannot disappear without an explicit
choice. Personal copies do not overwrite the source, and exports do not mix draft
inputs with saved values.

## Physical acceptance limits

This was intentionally a pre-release with limited hardware feedback. Printer
entries describe documented setups, not certification of every printer, reader,
firmware, plugin and tag. Qt/software tests do not replace native human acceptance
on every OS or physical NFC write/read-back and printer recognition.

[Compatibility feedback](https://github.com/ArN-LaB/Spoolscribe-/issues/new?template=printer_compatibility.yml)
should identify model, reader/connection, firmware/plugin versions, chip, format,
writer application, expected/actual outcome and any read-back evidence.

## Publication and preservation

User catalogue and Orca profiles were unchanged by preparation. Historical
fixtures were digest-checked; photographs/logos retained provenance and original
bytes. Local work traces and the separate QR work were not bundled. macOS was
ad hoc signed, without Apple notarisation. Windows had no commercial signing
certificate. Public archives/checksums were attached to the release.
