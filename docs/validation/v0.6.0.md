## Digital scope

QR carries the exact validated NDEF from OpenSpool 1.0/OpenTag3D 2.004 export.
SSQR1 uses Base45, raw/zlib, bounded length and SHA-256. The digest detects
corruption, not authenticity. PNG/SVG are standalone digital files, with no
server or personal-catalogue transfer. Companion development was separate;
printing, D101, Bluetooth, real phones, chip writing and printer recognition
were deferred.

## Limits and retained warnings

Independent digital decoding does not certify a camera, phone, tag, reader or
real firmware. The application was a pre-release while its digital SSQR1 contract
was stable. No exhaustive physical acceptance was claimed; existing print
checks did not qualify a label printer. macOS: Apple Silicon/ad hoc signature,
no notarisation. Windows: no commercial certificate. Linux: x64 with system Qt
libraries required. [Compatibility feedback](https://github.com/ArN-LaB/Spoolscribe-/issues/new?template=printer_compatibility.yml).

Linux build logs reported nine unresolved external Qt XCB/XKB libraries,
repeated across plugins; they remained target-system prerequisites. Offscreen
probes passed without measuring a native X11/Wayland desktop.
[Linux runtime notes](../DEVELOPMENT.md#linux-desktop-runtime). PyInstaller also
warned about conditional macOS CoreFoundation import; language/frozen probes
passed. The release action emitted Node `DEP0005` for `Buffer()`; six files were
nonetheless published and verified. Warnings are retained, with no failed job
or test concealed.
