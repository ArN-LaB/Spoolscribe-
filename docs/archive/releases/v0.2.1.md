## What changes for you

The logo area changes from a square to a separate width/height bound. Square logos keep their appearance; the historical technical record below retains the exact dimensions.

The following details describe this version at its original publication stage,
including then-current stable-version statements. They do not redefine today’s latest release.

##### Fixed
- **Brand logo rendering for wide wordmarks (ROSA3D).** The logo widget was a
  52×52 px square — ROSA3D's 334×94 wordmark was squished to ~15 px tall.
  `load_brand_pixmap` now accepts a separate `max_w` bound; the logo widget is
  160×52 px (left-aligned), so ROSA3D renders at ~160×45 px while square logos
  (Polymaker, Prusament) are unaffected.

</details>
